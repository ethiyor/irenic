Road Salt Analyst

Privacy and Gmail access

Updated September 18, 2026. Road Salt Analyst is a small research application operated by Yordanos Kassa. Contact: ytk2108@columbia.edu.

Signing in and connecting Gmail

Google sign-in verifies your account and email address. Connecting Gmail is a separate, optional consent step. It requests permission to read mail and send messages from the same account. We store an encrypted authorization token so the service can check for campaign replies while you are away.

Information the application handles

We store your account email, workspace membership, campaign contacts, prepared and sent messages, matched replies and their original attachments, schedules, and activity records. Mailbox checks inspect messages from the campaign period to match replies, including spam and trash. Only matched incoming messages are retained as campaign records. Google grants broader mailbox read permission because the application cannot request permission for individual campaign threads.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to operate your workspace: prepare contract requests, detect replies, prepare follow-ups and forwards, and keep an approval record. Campaign messages are sent only after an authorized workspace member approves the exact message. Forwarding shares the original reply and attachments with the recipient displayed in the approval queue.

New personal workspaces are separate from other users' workspaces. Existing invited members may access a shared campaign according to their assigned role. The application operator and hosting administrators can access the underlying service infrastructure. The service is hosted on Render and uses Google for sign-in and Gmail access. Gmail data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models by this application.

Storage and deletion

Campaign records remain on the service's private persistent storage until removed by the operator. Disconnecting Gmail removes the stored authorization and disables that workspace's schedule; it does not delete its campaign records or messages already delivered to recipients. You can also revoke access in your Google account. To request removal of your workspace and retained records, contact the operator from your registered address. Operational backups, if any, may require separate removal; messages held by recipients are outside this application's control.

Your controls

You can disconnect Gmail, disable scheduling, pause outreach, and review or reject pending messages. Sign-in does not connect your mailbox automatically. Do not add confidential information or recipients you are not authorized to contact.
